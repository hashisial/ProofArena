## Findings

- **53/53** dashboard children inherit platform authentication, email verification, and the existing dashboard shell.
- **37/53** have an explicit child role wrapper; **16/53** have no child role restriction in current code.
- Of the 16 parent-auth-only routes, eight have metadata and eight do not: `/account`, `/connections`, `/leads`, `/network`, `/payments`, `/projects`, `/provider-services`, and `/scraper`.
- Absence of a child role guard is unresolved product policy, not automatically a defect.
- No dashboard-shell or guard change is authorized.
