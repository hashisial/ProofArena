## Common Evidence

Every row is an active route leaf in `client/src/routes/AppRoutes.jsx`. The active role system uses `USER_ROLES` and `RoleRoute`/`RoleProtectedRoute`. Route constants come from the existing registry. Navigation visibility is advisory, never authorization.

## Findings

- Enumerated explicit role routes: **42**.
- Provider and client route guards prevent unrelated coarse roles at the browser boundary; they do not establish tenant or record ownership.
- The support surface is limited to its explicit dashboard route in this tree. Broader support access is not approved.
- Role hierarchy and `super_admin` behavior differ across frontend and backend helpers and must not be inferred.
- The 16 parent-auth-only dashboard routes are excluded from this matrix and remain in the dashboard matrix for policy approval.
